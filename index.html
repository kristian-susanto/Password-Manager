Zero-knowledge by design

PBKDF2-SHA256 with 310,000 iterations derives your key locally. Your master password is never stored — not even hashed.

One file, full control

Your vault is a plain CSV or JSON of base64 ciphertext. Back it up, put it on a USB stick, sync it yourself — no database required.

Offline & tracker-free

No network calls, no analytics, no cookies. Everything runs in your browser tab and vanishes when you close it.
